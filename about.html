About Calan

Artist. Educator. Visionary.

Calan Bryant is a choreographer, creative director, performer, dancer, educator, and presenter focused on developing artists through movement, storytelling, performance quality, and culture.

With over 10 years of experience across film, television, touring, concert dance, education, and commercial entertainment, Calan’s credits include Netflix, the NBA, TV One, national tours, and major recording artists.

Teaching Philosophy

Training dancers to understand musicality, intention, performance, confidence, movement quality, and full-body storytelling.
