About Calan

Artist. Educator. Visionary.

Calan Bryant is a choreographer, creative director, performer, and educator focused on developing artists through movement, storytelling, performance quality, and culture. Calan Bryant is a choreographer, creative director, dancer, educator, and presenter with over 10 years of experience across film, television, touring, concert dance, education, and commercial entertainment. His credits include Netflix, the NBA, TV One, national tours, and major recording artists.

Teaching Philosophy

Training dancers to understand musicality, intention, performance, confidence, and full-body storytelling.
